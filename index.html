Project Overview

MediMate

Your trusted companion for medication management.

Managing medications can be challenging, especially for older adults who may have difficulty understanding medication instructions or remembering when to take their medicine. MediMate aims to make medication information easier to understand and daily medication management more convenient. It also seeks to help families stay informed and support their loved ones' medication routines.
