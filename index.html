Smart Habits for Maintaining Health with Regular Medication Taking

Goals & Features

Project Goals

  • Help older adults manage their medication schedules more easily.
  • Provide clear and easy-to-understand medication information.
  • Help family members stay informed about their loved ones' medication routines.

Planned Features

  • Medication Reminders: Remind users when it is time to take their medication.
  • Easy Medication Information: Explain medication instructions, effects, and precautions in simple language.
  • Medication Interaction Alerts: Help users identify potential interactions between registered medications.
  • Family Connection: Allow family members to check shared medication schedules and records.

Success Criteria

  • Users can easily check their medication schedules and instructions.
  • Users can recognize their medication times through reminders.
  • Family members can check shared medication records conveniently.

Medication Assistance Service for Older Adults

one-sentence summary

An easy-to-use service that helps people understand their medicine, take it on time, and stay connected with family, all with simple words and big text.

short description

Many people, especially older adults, struggle to understand the information about their medicine, remember when to take it, or know which medicines are unsafe together. Families living far away also have no easy way to know if their parents are taking their medicine properly. While many people can simply search online, older adults often get lost in the flood of information, or even find it hard to access it in the first place, so our service organizes it all in one place and reminds them when to take their medicine.
